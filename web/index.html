      How this pathway works

      Every neuron you see is one node of a doubly linked list. The arrows between neurons are its prev and next links. You edit the list by editing the drawing.

      1. 1
        Select a neuronClick any cell (in the drawing or in the list) to place the cursor — the ringed, highlighted one.
      2. 2
        Operate on itInsert a neuron before or after it, lesion it, add myelin, excite or inhibit it, reverse the whole chain, or close it into a loop.
      3. 3
        Fire the signalSend the action potential forward (orthodromic) or backward (antidromic) and read the measurements on the right.
